## Spec

- On-screen copy draft: 227 characters; ~7 chars/sec plus one beat after each sentence
- Narration: none — on-screen words are enough; voice would turn it into a lecture

## Motion (stage directions, not on-screen words)

Same overhead paper throughout. Thirty marks present; a stamp comes down but marks remain. One pass clears ten; twenty stay. Before the next look, marks drop on the worker's side. Manager responsibility does not ride away on the stamp.
