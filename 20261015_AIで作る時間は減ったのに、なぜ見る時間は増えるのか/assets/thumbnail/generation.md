## 使用プロンプト

Use case: ads-marketing
Create one finished premium Japanese video thumbnail, landscape 16:9, ideally 1920x1080. Theme exactly: AIで作る時間は減ったのに、なぜ見る時間は増えるのか。
Input image is a reference for the video's actual graphite conveyor, glossy cyan balls and rectangular review gate. Build a striking editorial composition consistent with this reference, not a generic AI robot image.
Typography is the main focus, with extremely bold, impeccably legible Japanese Gothic lettering, clean accurate glyphs. Exact headline arranged as:
「AIで作る時間は」
「減ったのに、」
「なぜ見る時間は」
「増えるのか。」
Do not include the quotation marks. The first two lines form a smaller upper block, the last two lines are dramatically larger. White and vivid cyan lettering on near-black graphite background, last line in warm amber for tension. The text occupies the left 65%, spacious safe margins and deliberate line spacing, no overlap with busy imagery. Treat this as one coherent typographic hierarchy, not four equal lines. No other text.
On the right and lower right, an enlarged cinematic close-up interpretation of the reference conveyor: many glossy cyan balls accumulating immediately BEFORE the rectangular gate, a few beyond it. Clear silhouette, directional perspective and dramatic studio lighting. Balls represent work accumulating, not damaged or malfunctioning AI. Keep enough actual balls visible to read the metaphor even at small thumbnail size. Integrate the machinery into the dark negative space without covering the headline. Visual energy through a diagonal conveyor perspective, restrained thin directional marks, sharp high contrast and crafted depth. Attractive sophisticated motion-design video identity, not sensational tabloid.
No people, robots, brains, clocks, logos, tiny labels, charts, random numbers, borders or watermarks. Keep Japanese headline exact.
