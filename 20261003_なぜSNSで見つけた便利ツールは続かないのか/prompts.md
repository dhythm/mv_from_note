# 生成プロンプト

共通: locked tripod, no pan, no zoom, no cut. Same wooden desk, same 20-degree-above 3/4 angle, soft daylight from frame left, matte 3D motion graphics, no people, no screens, no readable text, no logos. Generate the action short. Timing to the beats is done by holding the first and last frames, not by generating a long clip.

## 1. 机（全カットの台）

Locked shot of a small empty wooden desk in a quiet room. Soft daylight from the left, matte surface, motion-graphics realism. Nothing moves. No text, no logos.

## 2. 積み（0:00–0:12）

Same locked camera and desk. Plain anonymous objects, blank cards, small boxes, a closed notebook, and a pebble-like device, drop gently and stack into one tall unstable pile. Only the objects move. No text, no logos.

## 3. 崩落（0:12の傾きから0:55の落ちまで。一つの動き）

Same locked shot. The pile leans, then most objects fall under their own weight and slide off the desk. One small plain pot stays in the center. No explosion, no camera move, no text, no logos.

## 4. 成長（0:55–1:15）

Same locked shot. From that one pot, a single plant grows branches that settle along the desk, slow and steady. Fallen objects stay still. No second plant, no camera move, no text, no logos.
