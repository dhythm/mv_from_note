# 画像生成プロンプト

## キャラクターシート

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished production character model sheet for an original friendly mascot to be used as isolated acting footage composited among kinetic typography. Landscape 3:2 canvas, generous margins, clean warm off-white background, restrained professional animation studio presentation. This is ONE CHARACTER shown consistently in NINE separated views in a precise 3 by 3 grid, not nine different character designs. No labels or any text, no grid lines, no logo or watermark. Keep empty gutters and do not overlap cells.

Character design: a compact cream-colored round-headed little maker with a warm, appealing personality, smooth matte soft clay/resin appearance, a plump simple torso, exactly two short arms with rounded mitten hands with NO individual fingers and exactly two short legs with simple cream feet. Large round head roughly 45 percent of total standing height. Two dark brown small vertical oval eyes, expressive but understated brows, tiny simple mouth. No nose, no ears, no hair, no fur, no antenna, no tail, no eyelashes, no transparent parts. Wear ONLY a plain sleeveless terracotta vest, clean simple V opening revealing cream at the chest, no shirt, no collar, no buttons, no pockets, no trim, no patterns, no lettering, no accessories. Muted terracotta and warm cream are the ONLY character colors, plus dark brown face. NO lime green, neon, blue costume, devices or props. Rounded silhouette, premium gentle tactile finish, subtle lighting that defines volume without a glossy plastic shine. This is an original character, not based on an existing franchise. Friendly and capable rather than babyish.

Nine cells:
TOP ROW, all identical full-body neutral pose and same scale: front view; three-quarter view showing the character's left side; exact left profile.
MIDDLE ROW: full-body rear view showing the same terracotta vest and cream head; face and shoulders close-up with hesitant worried expression (eyes look upper-left, small tense mouth); same face and shoulders close-up with a delighted discovery expression (slightly wider eyes, warm modest smile).
BOTTOM ROW, full body at same scale as top row: hesitant bracing pose with shoulders slightly raised and hands close to chest, eyes looking upper-left; curious forward lean with the character's right mitten-hand reaching down toward SCREEN LEFT to gently tap an INVISIBLE waist-high target, left arm close to body; absorbed tinkering pose with the same right hand adjusting an INVISIBLE target toward SCREEN LEFT, with an affectionate concentrated smile. Absolutely NO visible tool, table, button or target in either pose.

Maintain exact identical head proportions, eye spacing, body proportions, terracotta vest shape and color, cream hands and feet in every view. Soft neutral studio light, no dramatic cast shadows, no floor line or scenery. The rear view must genuinely show the back, not a face. Every full-body view must show the complete feet with clear margins. Clean silhouette suitable for later background removal. Do not include typography, arrows, color swatches or logos. High visual clarity and detailed enough to function as the canonical character identity reference.
```

## 単体の共通参照（シートを入力参照）

One character only. Same cream round head and plump body, same dark brown oval eyes and small smile, same two small brows, same plain terracotta sleeveless V-open vest, same short arms with simple mitten hands and two short legs with cream feet. No nose, ears, hair, fingers, tail, accessories, graphics or lettering. Preserve the approved proportions, materials and colors precisely. The head is roughly 45 percent of total standing height. Near-front three-quarter view facing slightly toward SCREEN LEFT so its right hand can later reach screen-left. Neutral relaxed pose, both hands low and clearly separate from the torso, both feet visible. Center the whole character on a plain uniform warm off-white background, body occupies 65 percent of image height, generous empty space around head, feet and hands. Soft neutral frontal studio lighting, very faint contact shadow only, no scene, no floor line, no props, no frames, no labels, no watermark. Crisp clean silhouette suitable as an image-to-video identity reference. This image is the character identity master, not a green-screen plate.
